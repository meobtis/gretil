# Satyasūtra

Satyasūtra

1 śrāvastyāṃ nidānam /  
2 ye kecid bhikṣavaḥ samyag eva śraddhayā agārād anagārikāṃ pravrajaṃti sarve te caturṇām ā*rya*satyānāṃ jñānāya /  
3 katameṣāṃ caturṇām /  
4 duḥkham āryasatyasya duḥkhasamudayasya duḥkhanirodhasya duḥkha*n*i*r*odhagāminī pratipad āryasatyasya jñānāya /  
5 tasmāt tarhi caturṇām āryasatyānām anabhisamitānām abhisamayāya atimātraṃ tīvra*ccha*ndo vyāyāmaś cotsuḍhiś cāprativāṇiś ca smṛtyā saṃprajanyena ca yogaḥ karaṇīyaḥ /
